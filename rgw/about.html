About

Some things are impossible. Some things really happened. And some things nobody has ever been able to explain.

Reality Got Weird is about the moment your understanding of the world slips — the "wait, WHAT?" Every video takes one idea, follows it all the way down, and tells you honestly which kind it is.

What If — impossible scenarios, taken seriously. This Really Happened — true stories that sound invented. Unexplained — the ones still open.

We label hypotheticals as hypothetical. We say when something is disputed. When nobody knows, we say nobody knows.

Fascinating without making anything up.

How the videos are made

Each short starts from one idea, researched against published sources. We write the script, produce the visuals and narration, and edit every video ourselves. Hypotheticals are labeled as hypotheticals, disputed claims are called disputed, and every video is original work owned by the channel.
