Reality Got Weird Publisher

The private tool the owner of the @reality.got.weird TikTok account uses to post that account's own original videos.

What it does

When a new short is finished, the publisher uploads that one video file to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API, with the caption and privacy setting the owner chose. It has no other users, no sign-up, and posts to no account but our own.

How it works

  1. Authorize once. The owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and approves the user.info.basic and video.publish scopes on TikTok's own consent screen.
  2. Read the creator's settings. Before every post the tool asks TikTok which privacy levels and interaction settings the account allows, and only offers those.
  3. Upload the video. The finished MP4 is sent directly to TikTok with its caption. Nothing is scraped, re-uploaded from other creators, or posted automatically without the owner.
  4. Confirm the post. The tool checks the publish status TikTok returns, then the owner reviews the live post.

Data

Access tokens stay in the system keychain on the owner's own computer. The tool stores no viewer data and shares nothing with third parties. Access can be revoked any time in TikTok → Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions. Full details: Privacy Policy · Terms of Service.
