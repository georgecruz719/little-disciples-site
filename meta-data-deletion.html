Data Deletion Instructions — RGW Publisher

Last updated: September 14, 2026

RGW Publisher stores no personal data on any server. The only data it holds is the Page owner's access token, kept on the owner's own computer.

To delete your data

  1. Open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business Integrations.
  2. Find RGW Publisher and click Remove. This revokes every token the app holds.
  3. Optionally email georgecruz719@yahoo.com with the subject "Data deletion" and we will confirm within 30 days that no data remains.
