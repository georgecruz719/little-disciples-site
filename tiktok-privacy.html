Privacy Policy — TikTok Publisher apps

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This policy covers the private TikTok API clients Little Disciples Publisher and Reality Got Weird Publisher. Each app is a single-operator tool used by the owner of the corresponding TikTok account to upload that account's own original videos.

Who uses these apps

Only the account owner. The apps are not offered to the public, have no sign-up, no users other than the owner, and no end-user-facing interface.

What we access

With the account owner's explicit authorization, the apps use TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API under the scopes user.info.basic and video.publish. They read the authorizing creator's basic profile and posting settings in order to build a valid post, and they upload video files the owner created. The apps do not read, collect, store, sell, or share any other person's data, any viewer data, or any analytics beyond what the owner sees in their own TikTok account.

Storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only in the macOS Keychain on the owner's own computer. They are never transmitted to any third party, never written to source control, and never logged. Video files remain on that same computer until the owner posts them.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted by removing them from the local Keychain. The owner can revoke the apps' access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions, which immediately and permanently ends all access.

Children

The apps are not directed at children and collect no data from anyone other than the account owner.

Changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
