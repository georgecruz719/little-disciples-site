Privacy Policy — RGW Publisher

Last updated: September 14, 2026

RGW Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Reality Got Weird and Little Disciples Facebook Pages to publish that owner's own videos to those Pages.

What we access

With the Page admin's permission, the app uses the Meta Graph API to upload Reels and read basic status of posts on the Pages the admin authorizes. It does not collect, read, sell, or share personal data about any other person, and it has no end users other than the Page owner.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer (the system keychain) and are never shared with third parties. No data received from Meta is stored on any server.

Revoking access

You can remove the app at any time in Facebook Settings → Business Integrations, which revokes all tokens.
