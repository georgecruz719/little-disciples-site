Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Little Disciples Publisher is used only by the owner of the Little Disciples YouTube channel to upload and manage that channel's own videos.

What we access

With your permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and update video details on the channel you authorize. It does not read, collect, sell, or share any other personal data.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared with third parties.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
